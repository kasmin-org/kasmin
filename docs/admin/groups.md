# Groups

*People → Groups*. A person can be in any number of groups; a group can belong to another one, and mailings to a group include its
subgroups.

| Kind | Members |
|---|---|
| By hand | added and removed on the page of the group, by imports, public forms or after payments |
| By rules | people who fit all rules (or at least one): age, membership, role, member of another group, number of past events; every rule can be turned around with *Not*. With an event, the age counts at that event and only events of its series are counted |
| Participants of an event | by status and role |

Groups by rules and of events are recalculated every hour, when they are saved, with *Recalculate*, and before every mailing. A group
without rules has no members. Memberships are ended and not deleted, so the history shows who was in a group, when and why.
